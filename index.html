Welcome to the Pokémon Memory Game!
- Select a Pokémon generation to start.
- There are 12 cards (6 pairs) shuffled randomly.
- Click two cards to flip them.
- If they match, they stay flipped; otherwise, they flip back after 1 second.
- You have 30 seconds to find all pairs.
- Good luck!
